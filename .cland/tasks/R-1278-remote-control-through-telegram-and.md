## Goal

Telegram and Discord become remote-control channels for cland: a gateway edge inside the hub mirrors the project's General topic and the human's needs-you rows into paired messenger chats (remotes), and accepts the human's messages, answers, approvals, photos, files and voice notes from them, with transcription through a provider seam. Spec: `docs/spec/v0.3.md` (complete, 2026-10-25). Vocabulary: Gateway, Remote, Pairing, Remote card, Transcript, Gap line, Outbox in `GLOSSARY.md`.

What the human can now do, by epic:

- **E-1307 The gateway edge and its authority.** The hub gains a gateway: an in-process edge on the dispatcher that serves each remote as the identity `human`. It adds no socket, listener or hub secret, and starts and stops with the hub. It only connects out, so a remote needs no tunnel, public address or open port. A remote may send into General or reply into the replied-to topic, answer, approve, and read `hub.status`, `task.list` and `inbox.list`. Every other request, pairing approval included, is refused with a reason. Messages, answers, approvals and ask outcomes from a remote record `via`, and both workbenches show it. Remote state lives in `hub.db`, with Discord ids stored as TEXT. A durable outbox per remote records an attempt before each send and resends only what provably never left. It merges edits, drops jobs whose source closed, and counts the General messages missed in an outage. A `retry_after` wait never exceeds 5 minutes. A poller lease keeps one gateway per project. A failing remote disables only itself, and the hub, the web edge and the other remote serve on. ADR 0003 names the gateway as an outbound-only edge of the one frame protocol (T-1327). QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-26-677-1307 (A-12342).
- **E-1308 Pairing, secrets and configuration.** Secrets live in `.cland/secrets.toml`, which is git-ignored, held at mode 600 and refused as a symlink. It holds `telegram.token`, `discord.token`, `speech.elevenlabs_key` and `speech.xai_key`. `cland secret set` reads only stdin or a hidden prompt, `cland secret list` shows names and 12-character fingerprint prefixes, and `unset` removes a name. No value reaches any output, log line or process listing. `CLAND_TELEGRAM_TOKEN`, `CLAND_DISCORD_TOKEN`, `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and `XAI_API_KEY` win over the file. They are on the sessions' deny-list, so no session and no speech command inherits them. One redactor masks tokens and keys in everything the gateway and the speech seam write. `cland init` writes the `[remote]`, `[remote.telegram]`, `[remote.discord]` and `[speech]` keys, `cland update` adds them, and `cland hub reload` applies them. Pairing is reversed: the chat shows an 8-character code, valid for an hour on Telegram and 15 minutes on Discord. The human approves it only from Neovim or with `cland remote approve`. Approval binds the numeric ids and replaces an earlier pairing. A stranger gets at most two replies. `cland remote status`, `approve`, `unpair` and `test` manage the remotes, and a rotated token keeps the pairing and mute. QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-26-677-1308 (A-12483).
- **E-1309 The Telegram remote.** The human's private chat with the project's bot mirrors General both ways. The hub polls with plain `fetch`. It stores each update before it confirms the offset, and counts only the paired chat and sender. General arrives with an "author · kind" header as Rich Messages, falls back to plain text with entities, and splits into numbered parts. The human's own messages are never echoed back. The human's text reaches General as their message, marked via Telegram. `/decision` and `/question` send those kinds, a reply goes to its message's topic, and 👌 then 👀 confirm storage and delivery. Progress notes and hub notices arrive silently. `/mute` silences only the General stream, and `/unmute` reports how many messages arrived meanwhile. `/status`, `/inbox` and `/help` are laid out for a phone. One request is in flight per chat, and a 429 holds the whole bot for at most 5 minutes with nothing lost or reordered. After an outage, late messages carry the time they were written, and one gap line counts the missed General messages. General gets an offline line after 3 minutes and a recovery line. A stalled poll restarts, and a lasting 409 shows as another consumer of the token. A 401 or 403 stops only this remote, with its cause and fix. QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-27-677-1309 (A-12653).
- **E-1310 Remote cards and approvals.** An ask arrives as a card naming who asks and where, with one button per option, "Other answer…" and "Decline…". Codex and OpenCode approvals arrive as cards with the truncated command and the harness's choices. An answer from a card shows as "approved via Telegram". Plans, requests, item-topic messages to the human and Claude Code's approvals arrive as 📥 notices without buttons, and a reply goes to their topic. The first answer from any client or remote wins and is stored before Telegram is acknowledged. Every copy of the card then turns into its outcome. "Other answer…" and "Decline…" open a reply prompt, and `/cancel` keeps the ask open. A typed reply to a card answers it. A closed card says "Already answered", a foreign press is refused privately, and a late message answers no ask. Cards arrive oldest first, and `/inbox` re-sends every open card or says "Nothing needs you". After an outage over 150 seconds, every open card is re-sent and the old copy is marked "↓ Re-sent below". The cards model knows no messenger, so E-1312 added only Discord's rendering. QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-27-677-1310 (A-12931). T-1357 counted under the Coordinator's waiver decision 6f1d3c54, with no green verify at its final reviewed head.
- **E-1311 Media, the upload frame and speech.** `artifact.upload` turns files into the human's artifacts on one message. It refuses a file over `[artifacts] max_upload_mb`, 50 MiB by default, and any file that contains a registered secret. The web composer attaches up to ten files through the web edge's HTTP endpoint, with its cookie and Origin checks. The web's artifact viewer shows PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP inline up to 10 MiB, and Neovim opens an image artifact in the system viewer. Photos and documents from Telegram become the human's artifacts on one message, with the caption as its text. An album is one message, stored before the offset is confirmed. Outbound images up to 10 MB go as photos and files up to 50 MB as documents. Larger files arrive as a line with the path. Each artifact goes to a remote once. A voice note becomes the human's 🎤 transcript with the audio attached, and the bot echoes it with its timings and provider. The speech seam has two providers. `elevenlabs` defaults to `scribe_v2`. `command` runs an argv with the audio on stdin and no remote secret in its environment. `provider = ""` turns transcription off. Download and transcription share one 90-second deadline. A voice reply to a card answers the ask through its transcript. Doctor shows the key's presence or the command's resolution without a paid call. `cland remote status` ends with the provider's data-use line. QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-28-677-1311 (A-13483).
- **E-1312 The Discord remote.** A text channel in the human's own Discord server is a second remote, and both can be paired at once. `/cland pair` gives a private code for 15 minutes, and approval binds the guild, channel and user ids. The client is hand-written on `ws` and `fetch`, with no new dependency. At start the hub checks for the Message Content intent and an empty Interactions Endpoint URL. A wrong setting or a fatal close code stops only this remote, with its cause and fix. The gateway resumes after a zombie connection, keeps identifies 5 seconds apart and warns at 500 a day. `/cland` is one global command with eight subcommands, answered privately and laid out for a phone. General is mirrored both ways in Discord's Markdown. Mentions are disabled, and splits are fence-safe at 2000 code points. Cards take buttons and modals, and a click is answered within 3 seconds. One gate holds across Discord, Telegram and both workbenches. REST follows the rate-limit headers, and a retried send posts once by its nonce. A restart within the resume window re-sends only the cards pressed meanwhile. A longer one reads the channel history back, up to 500 messages, re-sends every open card and adds one gap line. Attachments and voice messages become artifacts and 🎤 transcripts. Outbound files go once per remote, up to 20 MiB a file and ten a message. QA clear: qa-dod-2026-10-29-677-1312 (A-13649).
- **E-1313 Setup, doctor, onboarding and the workbenches.** `cland setup` gains an optional remote step after the hub and before the Aide. It offers Telegram, then Discord, defaulting to No even with `--yes`. It explains how to create each bot and reads the token at a hidden prompt. For Discord it checks the application: Public Bot off, Message Content on, no Interactions Endpoint URL. It enables the remote and pairs on the human's yes, then offers the ElevenLabs key with its data-use line. Without a terminal, an untouched remote reports `skip` with its commands and a half-configured one `open`. A declined remote is offered again on the next run. Doctor shows, per messenger, the token's fingerprint, the connection, the pairing and a stopped remote's cause with its fix as `warn`. It makes no platform or paid call. Both workbenches show a Remotes row with the connection, the pairing, mute, the outbox depth and the last error, red at once on a failure. Pairing rows show in both inboxes. Neovim's card approves them, and the web's row points to the terminal or Neovim. Messages, approvals and ask outcomes from a remote carry their source mark, and voice its 🎤. The onboarding tour has a ninth stage on remote control. The site has a Remote control page covering `cland remote` and `cland secret`, and the web-and-phone page has a Remotes section.
