## Item

E-2077 is a 0.4.1 epic under R-1880. It holds 9 tasks, all closed, in 2 stages, all closed. Its Definition of done (`cland task view E-2077`) has two criteria. C1: every child closed with its criteria verified or with a recorded reason. C2: a green Linux droplet cover and, at the epic's close, a green macOS cover at one dev tree that contains every child's landing. Every child has a landing commit of its own. The request names the two cover runs, their log artifacts and the commit they ran at. No product behaviour is tested here; each child was reviewed and verified on its own.
