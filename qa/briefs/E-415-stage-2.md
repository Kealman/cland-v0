The page is the live hub's web edge at `http://127.0.0.1:56935/`. Only that origin (with `http://localhost:56935`) is on Codex's browser origin allowlist (`~/.codex/browser/config.toml`, set by the Coordinator on the human's permission; see the amendment in `qa/briefs/T-1011.md`); `file://` pages and other ports are blocked by the Browser Use URL policy.
