# QA brief: E-418 Sessions, artifacts and accounts in the web (Definition of done)

This brief is the Definition-of-done demo of epic E-418. QA runs it in the human's place, at the full `dev` SHA that the request names, and plays the human's part as the DoD says: through the `cland` CLI (every browser sign-in link comes from `cland web --no-open`, and every session change is read back with `cland session list`) and headless Chromium (the suite's Chrome Headless Shell, contexts with their own cookies at 1440x900 and, for the narrow rows, 600x900, keys sent with their physical codes, the screen read from the DOM). It has three parts.

- **Part D, the droplet.** One demo lease at the requested commit, through `cland demo` only. On it QA installs the helpers below, builds the sealed environment and the web bundle, and walks the DoD's demo paragraph in four walks, in its order: the Sessions area with its groups, the Idle fold across a reload, the accounts strip, the queue view, the waiting session's command in its row and its drawer, New Aide and Launch a session from the header, Stop, Resume and Archive read back with `cland session list`, Capabilities, the session's page, the drawer's recent thread and composer, and Approve once in the drawer's card (walk 1); the Accounts area with every window, this browser's sign-in and the "finished" switch off across a reload (walk 2); the Artifacts area with a note, an HTML page and files opened, `k`, `d` and `s` (walk 3); the 600 px rows and a tap to the session's page, then `?` in the three areas (walk 4). It then runs the named test files that support each criterion. Once every droplet row and the captures are recorded, QA releases the lease at once (E418D-17).
- **Part L, local macOS.** The build gates in a clean detached worktree at the same SHA, the read-only live reads (the hold, the epic, its children and its plan), the typecheck with the web guards, the `web/` rules of `AGENTS.md`, the docs, the brief's lint, the Coordinator's epic cover (cited, never run by QA) and, immediately before the verdict, the freshness check.
- **Fallback.** If the demo start is refused for lack of a remote slot or spend budget, every Part D row runs on this Mac in the worktree, unchanged, after `quiet`.

Everything the run needs is in this file or in the files it names: whoever runs it cannot ask. Commands are for bash (zsh reads `$S:q` as a modifier, and sourcing the sealed `env.sh` from zsh aborts): start `bash` first.
