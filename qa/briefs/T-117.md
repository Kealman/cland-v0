## Item

T-117 adds `cland release --item T-n [<vX.Y.Z>] [--dry-run] [--json]` (`RELEASE_USAGE` in `hub/src/cli/release.ts`):

- It tags `v<version>`, taking the version from the release item's `version`.
- It writes the notes in this order, separated by blank lines: the text of the item's `## Notes` section; one line per epic child, `T-<n> <title>: <first sentence of its ## Goal>`; then the commit subjects in the existing format.
- It refuses an item that is not a release, has no version, disagrees with a version given on the command line, or has open descendants, and names the reason.
- On success it comments `Released v<version> at <full commit>.` on the item, as `human` when the human ran it, and closes it.
- Without `--item`, a release behaves as before and touches no item.

The `release` skill and the Coordinator clause release from the item. The walkthrough also checks the v0.1 tree: every epic closed on its own QA verdict (D16), the tech-debt items are triaged, and the build used what it shipped. The release item is T-130 (`kind: release`, `version: 0.1.0`, D18).
