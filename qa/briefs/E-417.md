# QA brief: E-417 Topics, threads and the inbox (Definition of done)

This brief is the Definition-of-done demo of epic E-417. QA runs it in the human's place, at the full `dev` SHA that the request names, and plays the human's part as the DoD says: through the `cland` CLI (every browser sign-in link comes from `cland web --no-open`), headless Chromium (the suite's Chrome Headless Shell, contexts at 1440x900 with their own cookies, keys sent with their physical codes) and a headless Neovim workbench. It has three parts.

- **Part D, the droplet.** One demo lease at the requested commit, through `cland demo` only. On it QA installs the helpers below, builds the sealed environment and the web bundle, and walks the DoD's demo paragraph in four walks, in its order: the Topics area, the thread, a folded run, history in pages and the read mark that Neovim then shows (walk 1); a fake Implementer's asks answered with `2` in one context and folded in a second, Escape and decline with a reason on `d`, the Inbox's sections, the leave rule and the archive (walk 2); the composer's kinds and keys, a queued message withdrawn, a draft across a reload and a hub disconnection, the approval cards and an HTML artifact inline (walk 3); the Events view and the last look that Neovim then reads, and the open-page notifications (walk 4). It then runs the named test files that support each criterion.
- **Part L, local macOS.** The build gates in a clean detached worktree at the same SHA, the read-only live reads (the hold, the epic, its children and its plan), the typecheck with the web guards, the `web/` rules of `AGENTS.md`, the docs, the brief's lint, the epic occasion cover and the freshness check.
- **Fallback.** If the demo start is refused for lack of a remote slot or spend budget, every Part D row runs on this Mac in the worktree, unchanged, after `quiet`.

Everything the run needs is in this file or in the files it names: whoever runs it cannot ask. Commands are for bash (zsh reads `$S:q` as a modifier, and sourcing the sealed `env.sh` from zsh aborts): start `bash` first.
