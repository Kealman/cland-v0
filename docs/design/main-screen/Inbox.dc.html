What feeds each section

asks: Ask messages from the Coordinator (the only role that may ask the human). Answered inline with 1-9 or a free text; the answer is a message with answer_to and clears the topic's "waiting for human".

approvals: sessions whose state is "waiting for approval". The hub does not answer them in v0; Enter attaches the session's TUI in the centre and the human answers there.

waiting for you: topics in status "waiting for human" that carry no open ask (the agent replied with a question in prose, or mentioned you).

notifications: journal events worth the human's eye: verdicts, role changes and handovers, session deaths and long idles, releases, imports. Dismissable; never block anything.

Order and sound

Sections are fixed in this order: what only you can unblock comes first. Inside a section, oldest first, so nothing starves. The top bar's "needs you" count is the sum of asks, approvals and waiting topics; notifications are not counted.

Where it lives

Right panel by default (␣n). Full-width centre view (␣N) when the queue is long, which is stage 2. Hidden in stage 1 when the count is in the top bar anyway. Always one keystroke away, never a separate screen you must visit.

Acting from a row

↵ opens the thing: the topic thread for asks and waiting topics, the attached session for approvals, the item for verdicts.

1…9 answer an ask with an option without leaving the inbox; i types a free answer in the composer with the topic preselected.

gi jumps to the item, gs to the session, gt to the topic, the same three motions as everywhere else.

d dismisses a notification; D all of them. Asks and approvals cannot be dismissed, only answered.

Not in v0

Timeouts, escalation, snoozing, grouping by item, and routing rules. The inbox is a view over existing state; it stores nothing of its own.

The three motions

Everything that names an item, a topic or a session is a link, and the same three keys move between them from any panel or view: gi item · gt topic · gs session. This is what makes the panels optional: you never need a panel open to get somewhere.
